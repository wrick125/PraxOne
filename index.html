Frequently asked questions.

What exactly does Prax One do?

We turn your long-form content into short-form clips and distribute those clips through a network of active clippers.

Do I need to create new content every day?

No. We primarily work with content you're already creating — podcasts, interviews, webinars, videos and other long-form material.

Do you guarantee viral views?

No. We cannot guarantee algorithmic virality. We build the system that gives your content more opportunities to be discovered and learn from performance.

How does the clipper network work?

Approved clips are distributed through active clipper accounts selected for the campaign and content.

Can I track the results?

Yes. Campaign performance can be tracked through transparent reporting and live performance data where available.

How long does it take to see results?

Timing depends on content quality, volume, platforms and distribution. We optimize continuously rather than promising a fixed viral timeline.
